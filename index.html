How to use InkCast

Write with the S Pen. Once the app sees the pen, your fingers only pan and pinch-zoom, so your palm won't leave marks. (Turn on Finger drawing in the ⋮ menu to draw with a finger.)

Erase with the eraser tool, or hold the S Pen side button while you write. The eraser removes whole strokes, never images.

Photo places pictures on the page. PDF turns each PDF page into a board page you can write on. Use the arrow tool to move an image, or drag its corner dot to resize it.

Record captures the board and your voice after a 3-second countdown. You can pause, change pages, zoom, and use the laser pointer while recording. Press the square to stop, then save or share the video.

Lines & vectors: drag with the line or arrow tool. Lines snap to multiples of 15° when close, or to grid points if Snap lines to grid is on (⋮ menu). While drawing, a readout shows the length in units (1 unit = 1 small graph-paper square), the angle measured counterclockwise from +x, and the x and y components. The readout is only on your screen; it isn't recorded.

Ruler & protractor: move them with a finger. Rotate them with two fingers or by dragging the round knob. Drawing along the ruler's top edge with the pen makes a perfectly straight line. The ruler shows its own direction in degrees. Drag the red arm of the protractor to read an angle. Both appear in recordings, but not in saved PDFs.

Save PDF exports every page (with your writing) as one PDF. Your board is saved on the tablet automatically.
